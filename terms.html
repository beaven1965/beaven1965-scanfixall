Retype uses artificial intelligence (Claude, by Anthropic) to read your page. It is usually accurate but can make mistakes, especially on crumpled, blurry or handwritten pages. Words it cannot read are marked [?]. Always read and correct the retyped text before you print, sign, send or submit it. You are responsible for the final document.
