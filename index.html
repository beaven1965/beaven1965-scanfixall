Which should I use — Clean or Retype?
✨ Clean paper (free): keeps the original page — letterhead, logo and handwritten signature stay — but faint creases or stains may still show.
📝 Retype (Premium): reads the text and prints it on a brand-new white page. Perfectly clean. You can keep the original letterhead/logo as a picture, and you sign it again.
